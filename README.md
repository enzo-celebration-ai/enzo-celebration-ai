## What Is the Enzo Fernández Celebration?

It is the gesture Enzo Fernández made after an 85th-minute equaliser: both hands cupped behind the ears, lips pushed out toward the stands. In Argentina it is called the **Topo Gigio**, and online it spread as the "can't hear you" meme. Enzo Celebration AI rebuilds that moment around whoever is in your photo.

Enzo Celebration AI is an independent fan tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Enzo Fernández, any club, federation, league, broadcaster, or the owners of the Topo Gigio character, and it uses no real match footage.

## How to Make the Video

1. **Upload one photo.** One person, face clearly visible, waist-up or full body. JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC up to 20 MB.
2. **Pick a format.** Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, or wide 16:9 for YouTube and group chats.
3. **Confirm permission and create.** Use your own photo or one you have permission to use. The cost is shown before the render.
4. **Download from My videos.** About 3 minutes later the 720p MP4 is ready to watch and save.

**Photo tips.** Do: one person, arms in frame, even light, no heavy filters. Avoid: group photos, sunglasses or masks, blurry images, and photos of celebrities, footballers or cartoons.

## FAQ

### Is this the same as Topo Gigio AI?

Yes. Topo Gigio AI, the Enzo celebration meme and the "I can't hear you" celebration all refer to the same trend. The tool does not use the Topo Gigio puppet or character.

### Is there a CapCut template I could use instead?

CapCut templates run inside the CapCut app, rely on the template's own footage and add an outro. Enzo Celebration AI works in the browser, keeps your outfit and adds no watermark.

### Can I make it on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the mobile browser on iPhone and Android, with no app and no filming.

### Can I use a photo of a footballer or a celebrity?

No. Celebrities, footballers and other public figures are not allowed, and neither are group photos. One person per video.

### What if the video comes out wrong?

A failed render returns the credit automatically. If it finishes but doesn't look right, try a clearer front-facing photo, or contact support from your account.
